# Experience Creator

An AI-powered tool that interviews you about your teaching goals and automatically builds a complete Primer experience — ready to use with students.

## What to Expect

You'll have a voice conversation with an AI interviewer who asks about your subject, learning objectives, and preferred teaching style. Based on your answers, the system:

1. **Designs** a multi-agent experience tailored to your goals
2. **Generates** original scene artwork to set the visual tone
3. **Builds** the full experience — agents, prompts, scenes, and flow — using your choice of roleplay or teachback templates

The whole process takes roughly 5–10 minutes. At the end you'll have a working experience you can launch immediately or refine further.

## Requirements

This module depends on two templates that will be installed automatically:

- **Roleplay Template** — for practice-based experiences with mentor, practice, and feedback agents
- **Teachback Template** — for explanation-based experiences where students teach concepts back

## Who Is This For?

Educators and experimenters who want to create conversational AI learning experiences without writing prompts or configuring agents by hand.
